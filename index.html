About

A quick look at how I think and how I work.

How I approach a design

  1. Understand the user goal (and the real problem).
  2. Map the flow with wireframes and UX structure.
  3. Prototype quickly so we can test ideas earlier.
  4. Polish the UI with spacing, typography, and components.

What “good UI” means to me

  • Readable typography and consistent spacing.
  • Clear hierarchy so users know what to do next.
  • Microcopy and labels that reduce confusion.
  • Layouts that stay clean on small screens.
